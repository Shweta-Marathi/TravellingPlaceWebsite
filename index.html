8 Must-Visit Places

Uttara Kannada is one of Karnataka's most beautiful districts, famous for its beaches, forests, waterfalls, wildlife and historic destinations.

01

Gokarna

A beautiful coastal town famous for its beaches, temples and peaceful atmosphere.

🏖️ Beaches 🛕 Temple
02

Murudeshwar

Famous for its magnificent Shiva statue, temple and beautiful Arabian Sea views.

🛕 Temple 🌊 Sea
03

Jog Falls

One of India's spectacular waterfalls where the Sharavathi River plunges dramatically.

💦 Waterfall 🌿 Nature
04

Dandeli

A paradise for adventure lovers with forests, wildlife and exciting Kali River activities.

🐅 Wildlife 🚣 Adventure
05

Yana Caves

Discover massive black limestone rock formations surrounded by the lush Western Ghats.

⛰️ Trekking 🌲 Forest
06

Mirjan Fort

A historic laterite fort surrounded by beautiful greenery near the coastal region.

🏰 History 📸 Photography
07

Sathodi Falls

A scenic waterfall hidden inside the lush forests of the Western Ghats.

💦 Waterfall 🌳 Forest
08

Karwar

The district headquarters known for beautiful beaches, coastal scenery and the Kali River.

🌊 Beach 🌅 Sunset
